2. Conversational Systems

3. Operations and Automation
Holds: back office that answers itself, admin-free week for service businesses, follow-ups that never sleep, spreadsheet rescue and accountant-linked upgrades.
Proof rule: one vertical intake to quote to paid flow, approval gate plus audit log, non-technical walkthrough on phone under 5 min. Current base: P1 agent demo plus P2 admin demo.

4. Apps, Web and Commerce
Holds: sites that close, get paid online ordering booking payments plus owner dashboard, customer portals and dashboards, native and cross-platform apps, MVP builds from idea to working system, rescue of broke builds.
Proof rule: live build, Stripe test purchase end to end, Lighthouse mobile 95 plus, axe zero critical serious, stranger completes order and menu edit from handover doc under 10 min. This is where app dev lives. No separate app card.
Current base: P3 ordering site plus resto writeup.

5. Brand and Identity Systems
Holds: identity that ships as code, brand plus live site, design systems Figma to code single source, launch and campaign kits, motion language.
Proof rule: OFY itself as reference implementation, one token change re-renders hub plus site under 5 min, stranger states what OFY sells for whom at what price. Current base: P4 brand hub.

6. Demand and Distribution
Holds: found in AI answers visibility ops, meetings-not-leads outbound engine with honest numbers, weekly growth queue, white-label growth desk for agencies.
Proof rule: self-run ledger on OFY 90 days, sends inbox placement spam replies meetings cost per meeting published, one agency resell at 2x wholesale 4 weeks. Current base: P6 engine plus seo-ops.
Copy note: never ship the AI marketing agency label.

7. Connected and Field Systems
Holds: industrial pipelines and runtime glue so notebook work still works on Monday, IoT device to dashboard loops, edge capture and QA, spectral and measurement flows, field deployments on bad network.
Proof rule: one device or pipeline to dashboard loop with logs and before after visual, fresh-clone runnable path. This is where IoT lives. No renders as proof.
Current base: Spectral plus open-ore-mapper, resto system writeup.
