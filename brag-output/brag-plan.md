# Brag Plan: oddfromyou

## Planning rubric (Step 1)

1. **What is the app?** oddfromyou.com is the studio site for OddFromYou — a design and engineering practice that builds brands, software, and production systems that could not have come from a template.
2. **What is the funniest or most impressive claim?** "if it could have come from anyone, it is not for us." Close second: "The internet is not running out of ideas. It is running out of difference."
3. **What is the visual hook?** The Oo mark — two irregular, hand-cut circles (red, then white) on black. Then the full-bleed industrial red field with the four-builder hero illustration.
4. **What should be shown from the actual UI?** Hero scene (builders illustration + "we are a team of builders with design & engineering taste"), the four work columns (PREDICTION / VOICE / INDUSTRIAL / CUSTOM with their halftone plates), and the contact line "bring us the part that doesn't fit."
5. **What is the shortest satisfying video?** 21 seconds. Four scenes. Restraint over speed.
6. **What tone fits best?** Preset: `polished`. Direction: quiet-premium industrial film — high-contrast red/black, exacting, slightly irreverent, never generic SaaS.
7. **What should the audio feel like?** Steady, clean bed (vol-12) at low volume. Sparse warm SFX: one drop on the mark, one soft impact on the work grid, one bell on the outro. No punch, no glitch.
8. **What should the share caption say?** If it could have come from anyone, it is not for us. oddfromyou.com
9. **What's the user flow worth showing?** Landing-page only. Visual journey of the live site: LogoIntro mark → hero builders → work columns → manifesto/contact close.

## What is this app?

OddFromYou is a design and engineering studio. The site is the proof: industrial red/black, authored type, motion that is structural, not ornamental.

## The angle

Not an agency reel. A quiet industrial film of a studio that refuses sameness. The Oo mark is the punctuation. The four practices are the work. The last line is the filter: if it could have come from anyone, it is not for us.

Specific because it uses the real mark, the real hero illustration, the real work plates, and the site's own copy. No "we build products" filler.
