Where California or another US state privacy law applies, the relevant section below explains your rights.

## 2. The Short Version

- We collect very little on this website. There are no accounts, no contact forms, no checkout, no analytics, and no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
- Our hosting provider processes technical request data, such as your IP address and browser details, to deliver and protect the site.
- Google Fonts receives a request from your browser for the typefaces used here.
- Your theme, accent, and cookie-notice choice are stored in your browser's local storage and stay on your device.

- You can ask us for access, correction, erasure, or grievance redressal using the contact above.

## 3. Personal Data We Handle

### 3.1 When you visit the website

- **Request data:** IP address, browser type and user agent, requested URL, timestamp, referring page, and approximate city/country derived from your IP address. Source: your browser and our hosting provider's request logs. Purpose: deliver the site, keep it secure, diagnose faults, and prevent abuse.
- **Font request data:** your IP address, browser details, and the font files requested, sent to Google Fonts when your browser loads the site's typography. Purpose: render the site's typefaces.
- **Preference data:** your theme, accent colour, and cookie-notice acknowledgement, stored by us in your browser's `localStorage` (keys `mf-theme`, `mf-accent`, `mf-cookie-choice`). Purpose: remember your preferences and choice.
- **Email data:** your email address, name, and message, if you choose to email us. Source: you. Purpose: reply to you and keep a record of the conversation.

We do not operate user accounts, contact forms, newsletters, or payments on this website. We do not knowingly collect precise location, biometric data, or data from children through the site.

### 3.2 When you enquire or become a client

- **Identity and contact data:** name, email address, phone number, company name, role, country.
- **Project data:** requirements, specifications, feedback, files, access details, meeting notes, and other information you share with us.
- **Commercial data:** proposals, statements of work, invoices, billing address, tax identifiers, bank or payment details and payment status. We do not receive or store full payment-card numbers; payments are made by bank transfer or through a payment provider.
- **Communications:** emails, messages, call notes, and documents.
- **Engagement records:** approvals, change requests, acceptance records, and correspondence about the work.

We ask you not to send special-category or sensitive personal data (for example health, biometric, financial-account, or precise-location data) unless we have agreed in writing that the project requires it and the lawful basis and safeguards are in place.

### 3.3 When we work on a client's product

We may process personal data belonging to the client's customers, users, or employees while building or operating a system for the client. In that role:

- The **client is the Data Fiduciary** under the DPDP Act and the **controller** under the GDPR.
- **We are the Data Processor** and act only on the client's documented instructions, under a written agreement.
- The client is responsible for giving notices and obtaining any consents or establishing another lawful basis for that data.
- We do not use client personal data for our own purposes, sell it, or use it to train AI models without the client's written authorisation.

## 4. Why We Use Personal Data

We use personal data for these purposes and on these bases:

- **To run and protect the website.** Technical logs and essential storage are used on the basis of our legitimate interests in operating a secure, functional site and our legal obligations. Essential storage does not require consent.
- **To answer enquiries.** We use your contact and message data to reply and to take steps at your request before entering a contract.
- **To perform engagements.** We use client, project, and commercial data to deliver the work, invoice, support, and manage the relationship under our contract.
- **To meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.** We keep invoices and records as required by Indian law.
- **To protect rights and prevent abuse.** We may use information to detect fraud, secure our systems, enforce our terms, and respond to legal claims.
- **Marketing.** We do not currently run a newsletter or marketing list. If we send marketing in future, we will do so only with consent or another lawful basis, and every message will let you opt out. Service, invoice, and security messages are not marketing.
- **AI and project work.** We process project data only for the engagement and only as the statement of work and our data-processing terms allow. We do not use client data to train general AI models without written agreement.

Under the **GDPR**, our lawful bases are performance of a contract, steps before a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, our legitimate interests in running and securing the business, and consent where we ask for it. Under the **DPDP Act**, we rely on consent and the legitimate uses recognised by that Act, including performance of a contract and compliance with law.

We do not make automated decisions about website visitors that produce legal or similarly significant effects. AI outputs used in client projects are subject to the human review and allocation of responsibility described in our Terms of Service.

## 5. Cookies, Local Storage, and Third-Party Requests

### 5.1 No cookies set by us

We do not set cookies on this website. We do not use analytics, advertising, social-media pixels, session replay, or cross-site tracking.

### 5.2 Essential local storage

We use your browser's local storage for:

- `mf-theme` — your light (red) or dark theme choice.
- `mf-accent` — your accent-colour choice.
- `mf-cookie-choice` — your acknowledgement of the cookie notice.

This storage stays in your browser. It is strictly necessary to remember your preferences and is not used to build a profile. You can clear it through your browser settings. Clearing it will reset your preferences.

### 5.3 Google Fonts

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser sends a request to Google for the font files and stylesheet, which discloses your IP address, browser details, and the page you are visiting. Google does not receive your name or email from us. Google's handling of that request is governed by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). If you block third-party requests, the site will fall back to system fonts.

### 5.4 Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel. Vercel processes technical request and log data on our behalf to deliver and protect the site. Vercel may process data in the United States and other countries under its data-processing terms and the safeguards described in Section 7.

### 5.5 Cookie notice

The cookie notice records that you have seen it. Because we currently set no optional cookies, the notice is informational; it is not a consent request, and acknowledging it does not enable any tracking. If we add optional analytics or advertising cookies in future, we will ask for consent before setting them and update this policy and the [Cookie Policy](/legal/cookie-policy) first. You can also use your browser's global privacy controls; we do not sell or share personal data, so there is no sale or sharing to opt out of.

If you are an end user of a client's product, the client's privacy notice governs the client's processing. Contact the client first for requests about that product; we will assist the client as its processor.

## 8. How Long We Keep Data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law:

- **Website request logs:** for the short retention period used by our hosting provider for security and diagnostics.
- **Local storage preferences:** until you clear them or change your choice.
- **Enquiries that do not become engagements:** up to 24 months after the last meaningful contact.
- **Client and project records:** for the engagement plus up to 24 months, unless the statement of work or a data-processing agreement says otherwise.
- **Invoices, tax, and financial records:** for up to 8 years or the period required by Indian tax and accounting law.
- **Legal claims and disputes:** for as long as needed to establish, exercise, or defend a claim, including a legal hold.
- **Backups:** deleted data may remain in backups for a limited period until the backup cycle overwrites it. Access to backups is restricted.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it so it can no longer identify you.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- HTTPS/TLS in transit.
- Access controls, least privilege, and multi-factor authentication for our accounts and systems.
- Encryption at rest where our providers support it.
- Vendor review and confidentiality commitments.
- Backups, monitoring, and a process for responding to incidents.
- Limiting access to client data to the people who need it for the work.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, and you should use appropriate care when sending sensitive information by email.

If you believe you have found a security issue, contact hello@oddfromyou.com. Please do not access, modify, or exfiltrate data, or test the site in a way that affects other users, without our written permission.

## 10. Personal Data Breaches

If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will:

- Contain and investigate it, and take steps to reduce harm.
- Notify affected individuals and the **Data Protection Board of India** as required by the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules, without undue delay and within the prescribed timelines.
- Notify a client promptly where we process data on the client's behalf, so the client can meet its own notification duties.
- Keep a record of the incident and the remedial steps.

Notification will describe the breach, its likely consequences, the steps taken, and the contact point for questions, to the extent known at the time.

## 11. Your Rights

Your rights depend on where you are and which law applies. We honour applicable rights without discrimination.

### 11.1 India — DPDP Act

As a Data Principal, you have the right to:

- **Access** information about the personal data we process and the processing.
- **Correction and completion** of inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erasure** of data that is no longer needed for its purpose, subject to legal retention.
- **Withdraw consent** where we rely on consent, with the same ease as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, and may mean we can no longer provide a service.
- **Grievance redressal** through the contact below.
- **Nominate** another person to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

### 11.2 EEA and UK — GDPR and UK GDPR

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, to receive your data in a portable format, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your supervisory authority. You also have rights about automated decision-making; we do not use personal data from this website for decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

### 11.3 California and other US states

If the CCPA/CPRA applies to you and to us, you may have the rights to know, access, delete, correct, opt out of sale or sharing, limit the use of sensitive personal information, and be free from discrimination. We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics. You may use an authorised agent to make a request.

### 11.4 How to exercise your rights

Email **hello@oddfromyou.com** with enough information to identify you and the request. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your data. We respond within the time required by law: generally 30 days for India, one month for the EEA/UK, and 45 days for California, subject to permitted extensions. Requests are free unless the law allows a reasonable fee for manifestly unfounded or excessive requests.

If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**, your EEA/UK supervisory authority, the **California Attorney General**, or another regulator with jurisdiction. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.
