# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Founders, product leaders, and ambitious teams evaluating a design and engineering studio for identity, digital product, motion, and AI-native systems. They need to understand the studio's taste, technical depth, working model, and fit quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present oddfromyou as an unmistakable design and engineering studio, demonstrate that visual identity and implementation are one practice, and convert aligned visitors into direct conversations.

## Brand Personality

Bold, exacting, and irreverent. The experience should feel physical, high-contrast, motion-forward, and authored rather than polished into generic agency language.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, interchangeable agency templates, soft gradients, decorative glass cards, timid typography, arbitrary asymmetry, and motion that feels ornamental rather than structural.

## Design Principles

1. Make every viewport carry one dominant, memorable idea.
2. Use a strict underlying grid so expressive artwork and typography remain controlled.
3. Treat identity, product, motion, and engineering as one continuous practice.
4. Prefer decisive scale and contrast over decorative interface furniture.
5. Make interaction fast, direct, and predictable despite the visual intensity.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast in both themes, preserve keyboard and touch navigation, respect reduced-motion preferences, keep controls clearly labeled, and avoid relying on color alone for meaning.
